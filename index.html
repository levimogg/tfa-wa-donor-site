Why It Persists

Funding has increased. Outcomes haven't.

Washington has 295 independent districts, strong unions, and deep local control. That produces innovation — and fragmentation. There is no single lever to pull.

The Insight

In decentralized systems, change does not spread through mandates or money. It spreads through visible proof that peers trust.

Our Strategy

Build leaders. Concentrate proof. Let demand spread the model.

We are not trying to control the system. We are building the mechanism that moves it.

01

Frontier Leaders

We recruit and develop leaders who deliver results in classrooms — and who stay to lead schools and systems. ~40 corps members annually, plus Ignite fellows and 1,400+ alumni. The talent engine behind everything.

02

Innovative Schools

We concentrate talent in partner schools where students experience agency, mastery, and future-readiness. These schools are the proof — real results in real buildings that neighbors can see.

03

Proof That Travels

When neighboring principals and districts see results clearly, adoption becomes safe and demand builds. Not because we push — because peers trust what they observe.

Research: Wallace Foundation — leadership as the key lever · Kraft & Papay — peer effects on teacher improvement

The goal is not to run the system. The goal is to change what the system believes is possible.
